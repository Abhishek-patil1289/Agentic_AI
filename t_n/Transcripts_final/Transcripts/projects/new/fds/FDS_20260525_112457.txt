EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The FreshBasket mobile application aims to provide customers with a convenient platform to order groceries directly from their mobile devices. The application will facilitate the browsing of products, management of shopping carts, and secure payment processing, while allowing store managers to oversee incoming orders and inventory. With a user-friendly interface and robust features, the app seeks to enhance the grocery shopping experience for users in Hyderabad.

BUSINESS CONTEXT
Project Background
FreshBasket aims to improve its service offering by launching a mobile application that allows customers to order groceries online, catering to the increasing demand for digital grocery services.
Business Needs
The need for a mobile platform arises from changing consumer behaviors and the necessity for FreshBasket to remain competitive in the grocery retail market.
Objectives
To provide a seamless grocery ordering experience.
To enable secure and efficient payment processing.
To facilitate effective order management for store managers.
Expected Benefits
Enhanced customer satisfaction and retention.
Increased sales through online orders.
Improved operational efficiency for inventory management.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Performance: The app must load product pages within 3 seconds.
Security: Must implement OTP-based authentication for secure user login.
Scalability: The app should support up to 10,000 simultaneous users.
Usability: The app must be user-friendly for customers who may not be tech-savvy.

USER WORKFLOWS
Customer Ordering Workflow
1. Customer opens the app.
2. Customer browses the product catalog.
3. Customer adds items to the cart.
4. Customer selects a delivery time slot.
5. Customer proceeds to payment.
6. Customer completes the order.
Store Manager Order Management Workflow
1. Store manager logs into the admin panel.
2. Store manager views incoming orders.
3. Store manager updates order status.
4. Store manager manages inventory.

BUSINESS RULES
The app will only support orders from the five specified FreshBasket stores in Hyderabad.
Customers must authenticate using OTP for secure access.
Store managers must have distinct access rights to manage orders and inventory.

USER INTERFACE REQUIREMENTS
Screen Layouts: The app will have a home screen, product catalog, cart screen, and payment screen.
Navigation Flow: Users can navigate between the catalog, cart, and checkout with a bottom navigation bar.
UI Behavior: The app must provide feedback upon actions (e.g., item added to cart).

INTEGRATION POINTS
Payment Gateway: Integration with a secure online payment service.
Inventory Management System: Real-time updates on product availability.
User Authentication Service: For OTP generation and verification.

ASSUMPTIONS & CONSTRAINTS
Assumptions: Customers are more comfortable with OTP login than email-based authentication.
Constraints: The app must comply with local regulations regarding online payments and data privacy.
