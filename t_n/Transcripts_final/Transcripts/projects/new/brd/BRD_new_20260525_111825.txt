1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
The purpose of this project is to develop a customer-facing mobile application that allows users to order groceries from FreshBasket, select delivery time slots, and make payments online. This app aims to enhance customer convenience and streamline the grocery shopping experience.

2. PROJECT BACKGROUND
----------------------------------------
FreshBasket Grocery Chain aims to modernize its shopping experience by introducing a mobile app that allows customers to conveniently order groceries from their phones. The need arises from the requirement to cater to a largely non-tech-savvy customer base while also providing store managers with a simple interface to manage orders and inventory efficiently.

3. SCOPE
----------------------------------------
In Scope  : Development of a mobile app for customers, including product catalog, cart functionality, delivery scheduling, online payment integration, user registration with OTP login, and an admin panel for store managers to view orders and manage inventory.
Out Scope : Any features not directly related to grocery ordering, such as email login, advanced analytics, or integration with third-party delivery services outside of the specified delivery time slots.

6. RISKS
----------------------------------------
  RISK-001: There is a risk that the target customer base may not adapt to using the app due to limited technological familiarity.
  RISK-002: There is a risk of technical issues arising from the integration of the payment gateway, which may affect payment processing.

7. ASSUMPTIONS
----------------------------------------
  ASSUMPTION-001: It is assumed that customers will prefer OTP login over email-based login for security and usability.
  ASSUMPTION-002: It is assumed that store managers will be able to effectively use the lightweight admin panel without extensive training.

8. CONSTRAINTS
----------------------------------------
  CONSTRAINT-001: The app will only support orders from the five specified FreshBasket stores in Hyderabad.
  CONSTRAINT-002: The project must adhere to the budget and timeline defined by FreshBasket and TechNova Solutions.
